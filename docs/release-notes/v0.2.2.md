# Lunlet 0.2.2

The notch entry leaves with the menu bar in full screen.

## Before you open it

This preview is signed ad hoc and not notarized, because no Apple Developer Program membership stands behind it yet. On a new installation macOS therefore asks you to allow it once:

- macOS 15 and later: open Lunlet, choose Done in the dialog, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section, choose Open Anyway next to Lunlet and confirm.
- macOS 14: right-click Lunlet.app, choose Open, then Open again.

Updating from an earlier version needs none of this: choose the notice in the panel's footer, or Settings › About › Check for Updates…. Versions 0.2.0 and 0.1.0 open the update window behind your other windows, so if nothing seems to happen, look for it there (Mission Control shows it).

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon; this build is arm64 only.
- Codex (CLI or app) and/or Claude Code signed in on this Mac.

## New

- **Out of the way in full screen.** When another app goes full screen, the entry beside the notch leaves with the menu bar, together with an open panel, and no cue appears over the app. Move the pointer to the top of the screen and the entry comes back with the menu bar; it stays while you use it.
- **Your choice.** Settings › Appearance › Hide in full screen offers With the menu bar (the default) and Never.
- **The desktop pet too.** The pet and its open panel leave when another app goes full screen and return with the desktop. It used to stay on top of full-screen apps.

To tell, Lunlet looks only at whether the Space in front is a full-screen one, whether an app's window covers the display, where the pointer is and how long ago the keyboard or pointer was last used. It never reads a window's title or contents and asks for no new permission.

## Everything else

What Lunlet does, what it does not do yet and what it sends are as in [0.2.0](https://github.com/yukunlabs/lunlet/releases/tag/v0.2.0); [0.2.1](https://github.com/yukunlabs/lunlet/releases/tag/v0.2.1) fixed updating from inside Lunlet.
