# Lunlet 0.2.1

Two fixes for updating from inside Lunlet.

## Before you open it

This preview is signed ad hoc and not notarized, because no Apple Developer Program membership stands behind it yet. On a new installation macOS therefore asks you to allow it once:

- macOS 15 and later: open Lunlet, choose Done in the dialog, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section, choose Open Anyway next to Lunlet and confirm.
- macOS 14: right-click Lunlet.app, choose Open, then Open again.

Updating from 0.2.0 or 0.1.0 needs none of this: choose the notice in the panel's footer, or Settings › About › Check for Updates…. Those two versions open the update window behind your other windows, so if nothing seems to happen, look for it there (Mission Control shows it). This version fixes that.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon; this build is arm64 only.
- Codex (CLI or app) and/or Claude Code signed in on this Mac.

## Fixed

- **The update window opens in front.** Lunlet has no Dock icon, and macOS used to open its update window behind whatever was on screen, so choosing the notice or Check for Updates… seemed to do nothing.
- **The update notice stays.** The notice in the panel's footer remains until you install or skip the update. Closing the update window used to remove it until the next launch.

## Everything else

What Lunlet does, what it does not do yet and what it sends are as in [0.2.0](https://github.com/yukunlabs/lunlet/releases/tag/v0.2.0).

## Files

- `Lunlet-0.2.1-macos-arm64.dmg`: the download; open it and drag Lunlet to Applications.
- `Lunlet-0.2.1-macos-arm64.zip`: the archive Lunlet's updater installs; not meant for manual installation.
- `SHA256SUMS`, `manifest.json`, `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md`: checksums, the build's provenance (source tag, revision, signing and notarization status) and the licenses.

Source tag: `v0.2.1`. Lunlet is released under the 0BSD license.
